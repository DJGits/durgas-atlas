Photo map — privacy notice
==========================

Publishing this directory, for example to GitHub Pages, makes every file in it
readable by anyone who knows or guesses its URL. There is no login, no access
control, and no way to retract a file that someone has already downloaded.

What becomes public
-------------------
* Full-resolution copies of every photo this build published (2 photos
  in total), copied byte for byte from your originals. Everything their EXIF
  metadata carries — camera, lens, GPS coordinates, timestamps — is published
  along with the image.
* Thumbnails generated from those photos, carrying no EXIF metadata at all: the
  Thumbnail_Generator copies none of it through, so no camera, lens, GPS, or
  timestamp tag survives into a thumbnail.
* Data files listing every published photo's coordinates and capture time as
  plain JSON. Anyone can download the whole set in a few requests and read it
  as a table; no interaction with the map is needed.

Coordinate precision
--------------------
Coordinates are published exactly as they were recorded, at full precision. A photo
taken at home therefore publishes your home's location to within a few metres. To
reduce that, set "privacy": { "coordinatePrecision": 0-6 } in the build config and
rebuild; coordinates are then rounded before they are written to any file.

Photos withheld
---------------
No photos were excluded by the location registry. To keep a photo out of the build
entirely — no image copy, no thumbnail, no coordinates — give it an "exclude": true
entry in the registry and rebuild.

Map tiles
---------
The map's background tiles are requested by each viewer's own browser, directly
from tile.openstreetmap.org.
That provider therefore sees every viewer's IP address and the areas and zoom
levels they look at. It does not receive your photos or their coordinates.

Not published here
------------------
Build reports list the original paths of files on your own machine, so they are
written outside this directory and are not part of the published site.
